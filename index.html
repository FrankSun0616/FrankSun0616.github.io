Profile

Physics, data, and everything in between

Trained at Macalester College and Brown University, now a PhD candidate at HKUST working inside the ATLAS Collaboration.

I am a Physics PhD candidate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, working at the intersection of experimental particle physics and machine learning.

As a member of the ATLAS Collaboration I contribute to Higgs boson measurements in the H → ZZ → 4ℓ channel, implementing decay STXS fiducial definitions inside the Athena framework and studying SMEFT coupling modifications through large-scale Monte Carlo generation. In parallel, I train a deep neural network electron identification menu on Run-3 data with the EGamma subgroup.

Before HKUST, I completed an M.S. in Physics at Brown University, where I built neural-network models for jet calibration with Particle Flow Networks, dual-stream architectures, and knowledge distillation. My undergraduate work at Macalester College spanned physics and mathematics, and led to published work in stellar nucleosynthesis, redshift survey science, and theoretical cosmology.

My research interests are Higgs boson properties and EFT interpretations, machine learning for particle reconstruction and calibration, and cosmology and nucleosynthesis.

Research

What I am working on

Two active ATLAS projects at HKUST, plus the machine-learning calibration work that preceded them.

Current

Decay STXS Implementation in the H → ZZ → 4ℓ Sector

Sep 2025 — present

ATLAS Collaboration (RML Subgroup) · HKUST

  • Implementing fiducial cut definitions for Higgs boson decay STXS inside the ATLAS Athena framework, extending the derivation and ntuple workflow for the H → ZZ → 4ℓ analysis chain.
  • Investigating SMEFT Higgs coupling modifications through large-scale Monte Carlo generation with MadGraph and Pythia, producing cross-section and yield distributions as a function of EFT operator coefficients.
ATLAS Athena STXS SMEFT MadGraph Pythia

DNN-Based Electron Identification for Run-3 ATLAS Data

Jan 2026 — present

ATLAS Collaboration (EGamma Subgroup) · HKUST

  • Training a DNN-based electron identification menu on Run-3 data to replace the current Bayes classifier, focusing on low-pT performance relevant to H → 4ℓ signatures.
  • Preparing training samples via the Tag-and-Probe framework and migrating the trained model from TensorFlow to the Salt framework for improved flexibility and ATLAS software integration.
ATLAS EGamma Deep learning TensorFlow Salt Tag-and-Probe

Previous

Neural Network Application for Jet Calibration

Jan 2024 — Nov 2025

Advisor: Dr. Jennifer Roloff · Brown University

  • Developed neural-network models for jet calibration, integrating a Particle Flow Network (PFN) to reconstruct jet constituents.
  • Performed hyperparameter tuning and validation to identify optimal configurations; implemented a dual-stream framework and knowledge-distillation scheme that improved calibration accuracy.
  • Integrated the models into the existing calibration framework and validated results against CMS open data, demonstrating consistent performance with minimal residual error.
  • Released the code for public use with configurable settings for multiple input jet data types.
Jet calibration Particle Flow Network Knowledge distillation CMS open data
